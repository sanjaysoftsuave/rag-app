# Duplicate charges on a card — Northwind Cloud

> Synthetic sample document for rag-app's quick start. Not a real company's policy.

A second charge for the same amount is almost always a temporary authorization
hold, created when the payment processor retries a declined transaction. It is
not a completed payment and no refund is needed: the hold drops off on its own
within 3 business days.

If the duplicate is still on the statement after 3 business days, contact
Billing with the last four digits of the card and the date of both charges so
the team can confirm with the processor.
