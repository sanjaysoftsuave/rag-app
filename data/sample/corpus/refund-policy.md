# Refund policy — Northwind Cloud

> Synthetic sample document for rag-app's quick start. Not a real company's policy.

You can request a refund within 14 days of cancelling a subscription. Requests
made after 14 days are not eligible, except where local consumer law requires
otherwise.

Annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused full months remaining.
Monthly plans are refunded for the current month only if no usage was recorded
after the cancellation date.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and arrive within
5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank. Usage-based overage charges are
never refunded.

To request a refund, open a ticket from Billing → Refunds and include the
invoice number.
